01 · OVERVIEWWhat is Not Collected

A small computer repair shop in Delaware and one green laptop on shelf 2. It was dropped off in April 2019 with a liquid problem, fixed the same day, and never picked up. The shop kept it. The world argued about it. The laptop sat there.

In September 2026 the owner came back with the ticket because the laptop got a token. The shop asked for an ID. This site is the shelf.

CHARACTER
One green low poly laptop with a two dot face on the screen, and a claim tag on a string
PLACE
The shop: glass counter, keyboards and cables, a wall of tagged laptops, the NOT COLLECTED shelf
THE STYLE
Everyone and everything looks like a 2003 console game, the shop stopped updating that year too
CHAIN
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

04 · CONTROLSUsing the site

LOADING
The ticket on the loading screen fills as the six frames of the shop arrive
THE WALK
Scrolling walks you from the sidewalk to the counter to the shelf, and the day turns into night. The frames blend into each other on the way
SOUND
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts the shop radio
COLLECT
The red button on the ticket. Press it as many times as you like, the line under it tells you what the counter needs
THE YEARS
Click a year to open it, ← → to move, ESC or CLOSE to close
CAM 2
The last screen is the shelf camera. The laptop blinks, the tag sways, nothing else happens

06 · PRIVACYNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. The shop keeps one thing and it is not yours.

MUSIC
One CC0 ambient loop on the shop radio, plays after your first tap
REDUCED MOTION
If your system asks for it, the dust and the blinking stop, the scroll still works
